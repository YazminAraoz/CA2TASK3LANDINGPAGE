Phase 1: Real-World Foundation

Initial Context & Human Insights

Operations: Tom Kehoe runs the garage with mechanics servicing around 25 cars a day. Bríd runs the office.

The Bottleneck: The garage receives roughly 60 phone calls per day for vehicle updates. When Bríd is busy, a mechanic has to stop working to answer them, which costs about 90 minutes of mechanic time per day.

Forgotten Vehicles: Cars waiting on parts often get overlooked, sometimes sitting for 5 days or more with no updates communicated to the customer.

Target Market: Independent owner-run garages (who would pay for a generic version of this solution).
