Raw Notes

Tom Kehoe services 25 cars a day. Bríd Kehoe runs the office.

There are about 60 phone calls per day for car updates. When Bríd is busy, a mechanic has to stop working to answer them, which costs 90 minutes of mechanic time per day.

Cars waiting for parts get forgotten. Some stay 5 days or more with no one calling the customer.
