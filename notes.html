Documentation / Notes

Raw Notes (Before AI Hunch)

CA

Tom Kehoe 25 cars

Bríd Kehoe runs office.

60 phone calls per day for car updates.

When Bríd is busy, a mechanic has to stop working to answer them, which costs 90 minutes of mechanic time per day.

Cars waiting for parts get forgotten. Some stay 5 days or more with no one calling the customer.

Internal app with three views:

  • Workshop: Every car in today, each with its stage (Booked in, Being checked, Waiting for parts, Being fixed, Ready to collect). One tap moves a car to the next stage.
  • Customer: A status screen found by job number and car registration, showing that car's stage and a short plain-English note, and nothing about any other customer or car.
  • Office: Cars grouped by stage. A car waiting for parts for more than 2 days turns red, and the view counts cars that are ready but not yet collected.

The workshop view has to run on a tablet on the wall with greasy hands: large buttons and easy handling, absolutely no typing.

Market for the generic version: Independent garages.

Who would pay for a generic version of this solution, and why?

Independent garages.

Process & Reflection

From my hunch I tried to add my insight to make it more freely. I commit and check the code, and iterate with my input with the AI. I checked for hallucinations, invented data, and testimonials.

I use Gemini and polish the prompt, commit a few times until I got the result I wanted. I learned from this exercise that AI gives you general info and sometimes due to communication misinterprets things.

Include headline, problem quotes, 3-step guide, pricing assumption (€199 setup + €59/month), and waiting list form.
